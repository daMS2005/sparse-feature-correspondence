# Superseded aggregates

The four within-story aggregates in this directory were produced with the same
planted-profile construction as the cross-story grid in `evidence/power/`, and
carry the same defect: the prescribed correlation applies to signed
coefficient profiles, not to the ablation-loss orderings the test measures.
See `evidence/power/SUPERSEDED.md`. Their detection fractions must not be
cited as achieved power. The shard hash inventory and the aggregate catalog are
retained as a record of what was computed. The within-story design was not
recalibrated with the corrected construction.
